## 🎨 QUALITY STANDARDS

### All Photos Must Have:
✅ Soft, professional lighting (no harsh shadows)
✅ Warm, natural color tones
✅ High resolution (minimum specified above)
✅ Clean, uncluttered backgrounds
✅ Natural skin tones (not over-retouched)
✅ Professional, clinical feel
✅ Relevant to beauty clinic context

### Avoid:
❌ Overly fashion/editorial style
❌ AI-generated faces (too perfect)
❌ Stock photos with watermarks
❌ Low resolution or pixelated images
❌ Inconsistent lighting between similar photos
❌ Overly posed or fake expressions
❌ Distracting backgrounds

---

## 🔧 IMAGE OPTIMIZATION

After downloading, optimize all images using:

1. **TinyPNG** (https://tinypng.com/)
   - Reduces file size by 50-70%
   - Maintains visual quality

2. **Squoosh** (https://squoosh.app/)
   - Advanced compression options
   - Compare before/after

3. **ImageOptim** (Mac) or **FileOptimizer** (Windows)
   - Batch optimization

**Target File Sizes:**
- Hero/CTA backgrounds: < 300KB
- Service thumbnails: < 150KB
- Doctor portraits: < 80KB
- Testimonial avatars: < 60KB
- Before/After: < 100KB each

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Consistency is Key:** Use photos with similar lighting and color tones
2. **Diversity Matters:** Include diverse ethnicities and ages
3. **Real Over Perfect:** Slightly imperfect photos feel more authentic
4. **Context is Important:** Every photo should clearly relate to beauty/wellness
5. **Test on Dark Mode:** Ensure photos work well with dark theme
6. **Mobile First:** Check how photos look on small screens
